Definition of brainrot:
Brainrot here means split-screen sludge: two or more unrelated videos play on one screen at the same time, for example gameplay footage (Subway Surfers, Minecraft parkour, slime or kinetic-sand clips) stacked with an unrelated TV, film, podcast or story clip. The audio often belongs to only one of the panels.
The following are not brainrot, even if the screen is divided:
- Reaction or duet videos in which one panel responds to the other panel.
- Co-op or multiplayer split screen in which every panel shows the same game session.
- Picture-in-picture commentary in which the creator talks about the main video.
- Single-feed videos of any style, including fast-cut meme edits, AI-generated characters and AI-voiced slideshows.
Decision rule: Answer yes only if two or more unrelated videos are on screen at the same time.
